Follow one ride

  1. 1Drivers stream GPS pings through the gateway into a Redis stream.
  2. 2Ingest files each driver under its hexagon (~0.1 km²). Late or out-of-order pings are rejected.
  3. 3A rider requests a ride. It waits in the rides stream.
  4. 4The matcher batches riders every 2 s, finds drivers in nearby hexagons, scores them by road time (OSRM + ETA model), assigns the batch jointly and claims each driver in one atomic step.
  5. 5The driver gets the offer and has 15 s to accept; otherwise the ride goes back in the queue.
  6. 6The console updates live: hex map, KPIs, and why each driver was chosen.
  7. 7Looking ahead, the forecaster predicts demand per area and the repositioner moves idle drivers toward it.
